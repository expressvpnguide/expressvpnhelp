ExpressVPN Help for Account Access

Account access is an important part of managing your VPN service. If you are unable to sign in to your ExpressVPN account, first check that your email address and password have been entered correctly.

ExpressVPN Help information may also be useful when dealing with password recovery or other account access concerns. Keep your password and verification information private.

ExpressVPN Help for Application Issues

ExpressVPN Help can be useful for application-related concerns. These may include installation errors, application updates, unexpected app behavior, or difficulties connecting to a VPN server.

Before seeking additional assistance, check whether the application is installed correctly and whether an updated version is available for your device.

ExpressVPN Help for Connection Problems

When ExpressVPN is not connecting or the application is behaving unexpectedly, begin with basic troubleshooting. Check your internet connection and restart the application. You can also try another available VPN server location.

  • Check your internet connection.
  • Restart the ExpressVPN application.
  • Try another available server location.
  • Check for application updates.
  • Restart your device.
ExpressVPN Help Tip: Keep your device type, operating system, application version, and exact error message available when looking for technical assistance.

ExpressVPN Help for Billing Questions

Subscription-related questions may involve payments, renewals, plan information, account settings, or cancellation requests. When reviewing billing concerns, make sure that the account information you are using is accurate.

Do not provide your account password or sensitive security information to an unknown person claiming to provide ExpressVPN Help or ExpressVPN Support.

Frequently Asked Questions

What can ExpressVPN Help assist with?

ExpressVPN Help information can cover account access, application setup, connection problems, subscription questions, and other service-related concerns.

What should I do if ExpressVPN is not connecting?

Check your internet connection, restart the application, try another server location, and make sure the application is updated.

What information should I prepare for ExpressVPN Help?

Conclusion

ExpressVPN Help and ExpressVPN Support information can help users understand where to look when they experience account, application, connection, or subscription related problems.
